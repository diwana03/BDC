# BDC v2.3.3-dev63

## Leaderboard Special Categories

- Adds Bachata Rising, Bachata Open and Bachata Invitational as leaderboard filters.
- Special-category rankings are calculated from published Final results, not from normal BDC division point buckets.
- Bachata Rising and Bachata Open use fixed leaderboard points 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for 1st to 5th.
- Bachata Invitational uses fixed leaderboard points 3, 2, 1 for 1st to 3rd.
- Leader and Follower rankings remain separate.
- Normal Novice, Intermediate and Advanced leaderboard progression logic is unchanged.
- Special-category leaderboard totals do not change normal BDC division eligibility.
