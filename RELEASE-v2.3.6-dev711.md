## Universal Dance Cup projector safe layout

- Places the BDC logo directly beside the event title and lowers the complete heading into the safe viewing area.
- Enforces 10 percent top and bottom margins and 5 percent left and right margins across every Dance Cup projection screen.
- Constrains Holding, Contestant Call, All Contestants, Judges, Scoring Progress, Live Scoreboard, and Winner Podium content inside the safe canvas.
- Prevents scoreboard status text, contestant calls, and podium cards from crossing or being clipped at screen edges.
- Does not change scoring, contestants, judges, rankings, results, or projection commands.
